🏛 SEC Insider Monitor — How to use it
Strongest signal: Multiple different insiders buying the same company in the same week. This is called a "cluster buy" and historically precedes significant price moves.

Ignore: Option exercises (insiders exercising pre-granted options — not real conviction). Small disposals for tax purposes.

Watch: CEO/CFO/Director open-market purchases with personal cash. Especially powerful when done during a period of stock weakness.

Time window: Insider signals work on a 1–6 month horizon, not days. Don't expect immediate price reaction.
🔭 Small Cap Hunter — How to use it
The opportunity: Large-cap stocks (AAPL, MSFT) have 40+ analysts and hundreds of algorithms watching every tick. Small-caps under £1B market cap may have 1–2 analysts. When news breaks, it takes the market days or weeks to fully react — that's your window.

Best setups: Small-cap with insider buy + volume spike in the same week. Both signals together are historically more predictive than either alone.

Risk management: Small-caps are volatile. Never put more than 2–3% of real portfolio in a single small-cap. Use the AI Small Cap portfolio here as a testing ground with virtual money before committing real capital.
⚠ Realistic expectations
Insider signals and low-coverage opportunities are real edges — they are documented in academic finance literature. But they work at a portfolio level over many trades, not on every individual signal. Expect 40–50% of signals to not work out. The edge comes from being right more than wrong over a large sample. Run the AI Small Cap portfolio for at least 3 months before drawing conclusions.
